# Contributions

Pull requests from users outside the maintainers team will be closed without review. We do not accept unsolicited code, documentation, or feature contributions at this time.

## Signing Your Work

We require that all authorized contributors "sign-off" on commits. This certifies that the contribution is your original work, or you have rights to submit it under the same license, or a compatible license.

Any accepted contribution that contains commits that are not Signed-Off will not be accepted.

To sign off on a commit, use the `--signoff` (or `-s`) option when committing changes:

```bash
git commit -s -m "Add cool feature."
```

This appends the following line to the commit message:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your@email.com>
```

Full text of the DCO ([https://developercertificate.org/](https://developercertificate.org/)):

```text
Developer Certificate of Origin
Version 1.1

license document, but changing it is not allowed.

(a) The contribution was created in whole or in part by me and I
    have the right to submit it under the open source license

(b) The contribution is based upon previous work that, to the best

    license and I have the right under that license to submit that

    permitted to submit under a different license), as indicated

(c) The contribution was provided directly to me by some other
    person who certified (a), (b) or (c) and I have not modified
    it.

(d) I understand and agree that this project and the contribution
    are public and that a record of the contribution (including all
    personal information I submit with it, including my sign-off) is

    this project or the open source license(s) involved.
```
